   source product ships. `docs/lift-ledger.md` records every lifted file.
2. **Spec 0.2.0.** `spec/lifecycle.md` and `spec/session-types.md` are new; service
   conventions gain §9's flag-defaults rule and §10 the problem document; gate discipline
   gains §9 for repos that run a pipeline. ADRs 0005 to 0008: plan format and critic,
   problem document, driver and chain, auto-merge and the reader.
3. **The pipeline in the scaffold.** Driver, chain, merge and reader workflows,
   `scripts/driver/`, and their fixtures, merged three ways from the extraction base. Every
   value an adopter may change is read from `.xal/factory.conf` by
   `scripts/driver/config.sh`. The plugin gains the reader agent, `/run-session`, `/idea`,
   `/explain`; the reader's evaluator is gate 9.
4. **The ops repo scaffold.** `scaffold/seed-ops.sh`: spec intake, the plan critic, status
   and the nudge, the cost ledger, six agents. Gate 10 seeds one and runs its gate.
5. **Deploy and monitoring.** `deploy.target` (none, fly), a smoke gate that proves §2 and

## Next

Phase 2: one options registry, `install.sh`, the `xal-factory` CLI (`init` with onboarding,
`seed`, `config`, `enable`/`disable`, `doctor`). Two settled design inputs from this session:

- The driver refuses `autonomy_level: unset`, so a factory that wants the driver on and
  auto-merge off needs `merge.yml` disabled with a level set, not an unset level. `init`
  must write the two together.
- Pipeline credential rows carry placeholder dates that hold gate 0 red until filled. `init`
  writes real dates when the person gives them, and omits the rows for workflows the person
  switched off.
